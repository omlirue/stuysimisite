Bringing care to the community, not the other way around.

StuySIMI is a student-led team helping run free community health fairs across New York City, with screenings and preventive care at no cost to the people who need them most.

Our mission
StuySIMI fosters community engagement for health fairs that support accessible, culturally responsive care for the communities they serve.

We're the New York City branch of SIMI, an organization dedicated to clinical, disaster, population, and public health needs. We connect students with the partners, clinics, and neighborhoods that make each fair possible.

Students help with outreach before each fair, follow-up calls after, and on-site support when schedules allow. Along the way, they learn how public health actually reaches people.
