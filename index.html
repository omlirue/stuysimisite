Care that speaks your language.

StuySIMI is a student-led initiative working with New York communities and health organizations to make care easier to reach, easier to trust, and responsive to the cultures and languages of the people it serves.

Our mission
We work to break down the barriers that keep communities from care, including cost, distance, language, and mistrust, by bringing care that is accessible and culturally responsive directly to the people who need it.

Too often, people go without preventive care not because it doesn't exist, but because it isn't built for them: it's too far away, too expensive, offered in the wrong language, or delivered by people they don't know.

StuySIMI connects students with community organizations, clinics, and neighborhoods to help close those gaps, through outreach, follow-up, honest conversation, and tools that meet people where they are.
