Expanding access to preventive care

Health Fairs

Community health fairs bring screenings and preventive care directly into neighborhoods, at no cost to attendees. We join fairs run by local health organizations and community partners, helping more neighbors hear about them, feel welcome, and follow through on their care. Click a fair for details.

How students help

Before, during, and after the fair

A fair only helps the people who know about it, feel comfortable attending, and get connected to care afterward. That's where students come in, working alongside the organizations that run each fair.

  1. Outreach

    Spreading the word through community networks, schools, and social media, in ways that reach neighbors in their own languages and spaces.

  2. Day-of support

    Helping on-site with coordination when the fair's timing and location work with students' school schedules.

  3. Follow-up

    Calls and messages after the fair so attendees are connected to the next step in their care.

Running a fair that needs volunteers?

We're glad to support health organizations with student outreach and follow-up. Students who want to get involved are welcome to reach out too.
