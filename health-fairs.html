Health Fairs

Each fair brings screenings and preventive care directly into a neighborhood, with no fees for attendees. New fairs in New York are planned on a regular basis with the SUNY College of Optometry and other partners. Click a fair for details.

How students help

Before, during, and after the fair

Many fairs happen during the school day, so most student work happens around the event, and that work is what makes a fair reach people and lead to real care.

  1. Outreach

    Spreading the word through community networks, schools, and social media so neighbors know the fair is coming.

  2. Day-of support

    Helping on-site with coordination when the fair fits students' schedules.

  3. Follow-up

    Calls and messages after the fair so attendees are connected to the next step in their care.
