About us

Students building trust in care, across New York

StuySIMI is a student-led initiative working to make care accessible and culturally responsive for communities, and to get young people involved in that work, from New York's boroughs to California.

Our story

StuySIMI began as part of SIMI's Students for Health Impact coalition, inspired by SIMI's community health work in California. We continue to support SIMI's fairs in Arcadia remotely.

In New York, we've grown into our own work. We build partnerships directly with local organizations, starting with the SUNY College of Optometry, One Hundred Black Men of New York, and Healthfirst in Harlem, around a shared goal: helping communities reach care they can trust.

That goal shapes everything we do. Traditional barriers to care include cost, distance, language, and a lack of trust built over time. We focus on care that is culturally responsive, outreach that meets people where they are, and follow-up that keeps them connected.
