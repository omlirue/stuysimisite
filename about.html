About us

From California fairs to New York neighborhoods

StuySIMI is the New York City branch of SIMI, built to get young people involved in community health, borough by borough.

Our story

StuySIMI grew out of SIMI's work in California, where SIMI's community health fairs bring screenings and preventive care to local residents. StuySIMI is part of SIMI's Students for Health Impact coalition, which brings students into that work.

We brought the model to New York to build community engagement among young people for these health causes across the boroughs, in ways that are culturally responsive to the communities each fair serves.

Today we're building new partnerships in New York, starting with the SUNY College of Optometry, One Hundred Black Men of New York, and Healthfirst in Harlem. At the same time, we continue to support SIMI's existing fairs in Arcadia remotely.

  1. SIMI in California

    Community health fairs in Arcadia with partners including Kaiser Permanente, USC Arcadia Hospital, and Huntington Health.

  2. Students for Health Impact

    A coalition bringing students into SIMI's community health work.

  3. StuySIMI in New York

    New partnerships, starting with a community health fair in Harlem, and an in-school discussion series.

  4. Across the boroughs

    Next, growing with students and schools citywide.
