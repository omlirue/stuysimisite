The Health + Tech Series

Community Discussions

Conversations with physicians, researchers, and founders on how technology and innovation, from digital health to new therapies, can serve community health in ways that are culturally attentive and reach the people who need them most.

Why we host these

Technology that works for every community

New tools in medicine, like remote monitoring, AI-assisted diagnosis, and digital health platforms, can bring care to more people than ever. But they only close gaps if they're designed with communities in mind: available in the right languages, accessible without expensive devices, and built to earn people's confidence.

These discussions help students understand where health care and technology are heading, and how to make sure that progress reaches underserved communities instead of leaving them behind.

Schools and organizations interested in running similar conversations are welcome to reach out. We're happy to share how we organize ours.
