Strengthening community trust

The Health + Tech Series

Conversations with physicians, researchers, and founders on how care and technology can earn a community's trust, by respecting the cultures, languages, and lived experiences of the people they're meant to serve.

Why we host these

Trust starts with understanding

Traditional barriers to care aren't only about cost or distance. They're also about whether people feel understood. These discussions help students learn how health disparities form, why communities may hesitate to seek care, and how new technology can close gaps instead of widening them.

Schools and organizations interested in running similar conversations are welcome to reach out. We're happy to share how we organize ours.
