Community Discussions

The Health + Tech Series

Conversations with physicians, researchers, and founders on how technology and innovation can serve community health, in ways that are attentive to the cultures and needs of the people they reach.

Why we host these

Starting conversations about communities

Alongside the fairs, StuySIMI leads an initiative at school to talk about health disparities, care gaps, and the role new technology can play in closing them, and about how to make sure that technology works for every community.

Schools interested in running a similar series are welcome to reach out. We're happy to share how we organize ours.
